# AI Usage

AI assistance (Claude) was used during this submission for the following:

- Generating and iterating on the C4 Context and Container diagrams, 
  including reviewing adherence to C4 model rules as taught in class.
- Reviewing and correcting arrow directionality and relationship 
  descriptions in diagrams.
- Generating UML Sequence Diagram helper visuals and verifying coverage of 
  all protocol messages (HL, GS, SS, ACC, REF, SU, OK, ERR).
- Drafting the design documentation text for the README, including 
  justifications for architectural decisions.

All design decisions were validated and adapted by the team. The final 
diagrams were created by the team in draw.io based on the reviewed designs.
